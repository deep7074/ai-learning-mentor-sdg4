# PPT OUTLINE – AI Learning Mentor

## Slide 1 – Title
**AI Learning Mentor**
SDG 4 – Quality Education

## Slide 2 – Problem
- Students may struggle with difficult topics.
- Assignments can accumulate.
- Study routines may become irregular.
- Students may not have access to personal tutoring.

## Slide 3 – Proposed Solution
An LLM-powered AI Learning Mentor that:
- answers questions,
- creates study plans,
- generates quizzes,
- tracks progress.

## Slide 4 – SDG Connection
**SDG 4 – Quality Education**
The project supports personalized and accessible learning.

## Slide 5 – Technology
- Python
- Streamlit
- Ollama / LLM
- SQLite
- Requests
- Prompt engineering

## Slide 6 – System Architecture
Student → Streamlit → LLM + Engagement Rules → SQLite

## Slide 7 – Main Features
1. Student profile
2. Engagement-support indicator
3. AI chat mentor
4. Study planner
5. AI quiz
6. Progress tracking

## Slide 8 – LLM Role
- Natural-language explanation
- Personalized planning
- Quiz generation
- Conversational learning support

## Slide 9 – Demo
Show:
1. Save profile
2. Ask mentor
3. Generate plan
4. Generate quiz
5. Submit quiz
6. Show progress

## Slide 10 – Limitations
- LLM can make mistakes.
- Prototype indicator is not a validated dropout predictor.
- Real deployment requires privacy and security.

## Slide 11 – Future Scope
- RAG with college notes
- Bengali/English support
- Teacher dashboard
- Attendance integration
- Notifications
- Better evaluation

## Slide 12 – Conclusion
AI can provide accessible, personalized academic support and help students build better learning habits.
